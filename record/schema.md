# Record Sheet

One Google Sheet is the system's memory. Chats start from it, never from their own memory.

| Tab | One row per | Columns |
|---|---|---|
| Runs | job run | date, time, job, model, tokens, points, result, output link |
| Outputs | thing made | date, job, what it is, link, check result, checked by |
| Decisions | decision by the person | date and time, decision in their words, what it changes, applied by, applied at |
| Misses | miss the person caught | date, what happened, first cause, job or prompt at fault, fix, fixed at |
| Open items | thing owed | item, owner, day and time, status |
| To Review | question for the person | question with full context, choices as actions, answer, acted on at |
| Scoreboard | day | misses caught by the person, repeats, oath breaks, outputs passed, outputs failed, usage |
| Jobs | scheduled job | name, schedule, model, reads, writes, overhead or production, status |
